A make.fun entry for [The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/c/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3): one funny, true line reviewing the dullest everyday object I could name.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, no randomness) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`. The line is 165 characters, one line, UTF-8.

## The entry

> Five stars. The rubber band held my cables together for two years, then snapped without warning and now lies on the floor as a tiny beige corpse nobody will pick up.

Why this object: a rubber band is as boring and everyday as it gets, and the joke comes from how it really behaves — it holds things together for years, snaps without warning, dries out, and ends up on the floor where nobody bothers to pick it up.
